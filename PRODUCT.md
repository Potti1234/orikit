# Product

## Register

product

## Users

OriKit is for TypeScript teams, Foldkit users, NativeScript teams, and
AI-assisted developers who want predictable application behavior while still
shipping real native controls. They need examples that are easy to understand,
test, inspect, and extend across web, Android, and eventually iOS.

## Product Purpose

OriKit demonstrates that one schema-validated Elm-style Program can own
application behavior while separate idiomatic views serve each platform. The
Todo slice succeeds when ordinary task management feels natural on web and
Android, while the shared Model, Messages, Commands, Stories, and canonical
traces remain visibly correct.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, native. The experience should feel quietly capable and
trustworthy, with the restraint and clarity associated with Apple's product
design system.

## Anti-references

Do not make the application look like a framework demo, generic admin
dashboard, card-grid template, WebView wrapper, or decorative AI-generated
concept. Avoid gratuitous gradients, glass effects, novelty controls, dense
developer diagnostics in the primary workflow, and lowest-common-denominator
cross-platform styling.

## Design Principles

1. Let the task be the interface: adding, reviewing, completing, editing, and
   deleting Todos should remain immediately understandable.
2. Preserve platform familiarity: share behavior, not forced pixels or
   browser-shaped controls.
3. Make correctness visible through dependable states, clear recovery, and
   deterministic tests rather than explanatory clutter.
4. Prefer quiet precision over decoration: hierarchy, typography, spacing,
   and feedback must earn their place.
5. Keep the implementation legible enough that an AI agent or contributor can
   trace every interaction back to a typed Message.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use named native and web controls, logical focus order, keyboard operation,
visible focus, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, and semantic
loading, failure, checked, and disabled states. Android work includes
accessibility-tree inspection and TalkBack-active verification. Future iOS
claims require VoiceOver evidence on macOS and an iPhone.
